# Consent and Compliance in Notifications

Compliance with GDPR, DPDP (India), CAN-SPAM, and TRAI telecom regulations.

---

## 1. Statutory Compliance Requirements

1. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP Act - India)**:
   - Explicit, affirmative consent must be recorded before sending promotional communications.
   - Consent withdrawal must be as easy as granting consent; opting out via SMS (`STOP`) or Email must take effect within 24 hours.
2. **TRAI National Do-Not-Call (NDNC) Registry**:
   - Marketing SMS messages must strictly check destination phone numbers against the active scrubbing registry before transmission.
3. **Audit Trail**:
   - Every marketing message dispatch references the timestamp and IP address where customer consent was originally captured.
