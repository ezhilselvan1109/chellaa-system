# Email Channel Specification

Provider adapters, deliverability standards, and attachment handling.

---

## 1. Provider Adapter (AWS SES / SendGrid)

- **Engine**: AWS Simple Email Service (SES) with fallback adapter for SendGrid.
- **Authentication**: Domain keys configured with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records to prevent spoofing and ensure high inbox placement.
- **Attachments**: PDF tax invoices generated by Order Service are attached directly to order confirmation emails via S3 pre-signed streams.
- **Bounce & Complaint Webhooks**: Ingests SNS bounce notifications; emails with permanent hard bounces are flagged in `customer-service` to suppress future dispatches.
