# Transactional vs Marketing Notifications

Legal classification, suppression logic, and frequency capping rules.

---

## 1. Classification Rules

| Attribute | Transactional | Marketing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Examples** | Order Paid, OTP, Shipped, Refunded | Flash Sale, Price Drop, Back In Stock |
| **Customer Consent** | Mandatory (Inherent to Contract) | Explicit Opt-In Required |
| **Quiet Hours** | Bypassed (Sent 24/7 immediately) | Strictly Suppressed 21:00 - 09:00 |
| **Frequency Cap** | None (Sent per business transaction) | Max 1 SMS/WhatsApp per customer per 7 days |
| **Unsubscribe Link** | Not required | Mandatory 1-click unsubscribe header |
