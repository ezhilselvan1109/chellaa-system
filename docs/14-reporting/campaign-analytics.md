# Campaign and Promotion Analytics

Metrics for promotional discount performance, ROI, and coupon conversion.

---

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Promotion Conversion Rate**: $\frac{\text{Orders with Promotion Applied}}{\text{Unique Cart Sessions with Promotion Viewed}} \times 100\%$.
- **Average Order Value (AOV) Uplift**: Comparison of AOV for orders redeeming coupons versus non-promotional baseline orders.
- **Discount Subsidy Cost**: Total monetary value granted across all redemptions against the campaign budget ceiling.
- **Redemption Velocity**: Hourly redemption count tracking flash-sale stock exhaustion.
