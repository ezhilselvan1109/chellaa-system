# Customer Analytics and Cohort Retention

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV), cohort retention tables, and churn analysis.

---

## 1. Metrics & Cohort Modeling

- **Cohort Retention Matrix**: Customers grouped by registration month (e.g. October 2026 Cohort). Tracks percentage of cohort placing repeat orders in Month 1, Month 2, Month 3.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Cumulative net revenue generated by customer across all lifetime orders.
- **RFM Segmentation**: Recency (days since last order), Frequency (total lifetime orders), Monetary Value (total spend) scores used to segment VIP shoppers and re-engage lapsed customers.
